### Basic Operations:
Math.abs(a): Returns the absolute value of a.
Math.max(a, b): Returns the larger of two numbers.
Math.min(a, b): Returns the smaller of two numbers.

### Exponents & Roots:
Math.pow(a, b): Returns \(a^{b}\).
Math.sqrt(a): Returns the square root of a.
Math.cbrt(a): Returns the cube root of a.

### Rounding:
Math.round(a): Rounds to the closest integer.
Math.ceil(a): Returns the smallest integer ≥ a.
Math.floor(a): Returns the largest integer ≤ a.

### Trigonometry (angles in radians):
Math.sin(a), Math.cos(a), Math.tan(a).
Math.toRadians(ang): Converts degrees to radians.
Math.toDegrees(ang): Converts radians to degrees
